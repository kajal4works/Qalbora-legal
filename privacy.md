# Privacy Policy for Qalbora

**Effective Date:** October 26, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 26, 2026

At **Qalbora** ("we," "our," or "us"), your privacy is a sacred trust. Qalbora is built on a strict **offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture**. We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data, nor do we display third-party advertisements.

This Privacy Policy explains how Qalbora handles information when you use our mobile and tablet application ("App").

---

### 1. Our Sacred Privacy Pledge
Your daily worship, Quran reading habits, prayer logs, and questions asked to the Islamic AI Companion are personal. All core calculations, study queries, and habit tracking are processed and stored locally on your device.

### 2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device
Qalbora requires certain device permissions to function properly. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to external marketing servers:

* **Location Data (Optional / Local Processing Only):** If you grant location permission, Qalbora uses your device's GPS coordinates solely to calculate accurate daily prayer times (via our Local On-Device Astronomical Engine) and determine the Qibla compass bearing. Your coordinates are processed locally on your device and are never tracked, stored on external servers, or shared with third parties. You may also manually select a city without granting GPS access.
* **On-Device AI Study Queries:** Questions submitted to the Qalbora Islamic AI Companion and answers generated via our On-Device Verified RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) engine are processed locally on your device. We do not log, read, or profile your religious questions.
* **User Preferences & Progress Data:** Your Juz reading progress, bookmarked verses, Prayer Tracker logs (Salah and Qada habits), quiz scores, and Mosque Iqamah overrides are stored locally on your device (or within your personal iCloud storage if you have iOS/iPadOS system backups enabled).

### 3. Information We Do Not Collect
* **No Account Required:** You do not need to register an account, provide your name, phone number, or email address to use Qalbora.
* **No Third-Party Ad Trackers:** Qalbora contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or data brokers.

### 4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions
If you choose to upgrade to Qalbora's premium features (such as unlimited AI Companion queries or full Islamic Knowledge Quizzes), transactions are processed directly by **Apple Inc.** via the App Store (StoreKit).
* We do not have access to your credit card numbers, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.
* We only receive an anonymized cryptographic receipt/token from Apple to verify your active subscription status on your device.

### 5. Audio Downloads & Network Requests
While Qalbora operates primarily offline, streaming or downloading Quran audio recitations (e.g., Mishary Rashid Alafasy) requires a standard internet connection to fetch audio files. Standard IP address routing occurs solely to deliver the requested audio file to your device, without user profiling.

### 6. Children’s Privacy
Qalbora is safe for users of all ages and complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international equivalents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

### 7. Your Rights & Data Deletion
Because all of your personal app data (bookmarks, prayer logs, AI chat history, and settings) is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it:
* You can clear your AI Companion chat history at any time using the trash/delete icon inside the AI Companion tab.
* Uninstalling the Qalbora App from your device permanently deletes all locally stored app data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new app features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
